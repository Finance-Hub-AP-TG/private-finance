Our Story

A platform born from a real market gap

Across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, private finance has long depended on personal contacts and word of mouth — leaving borrowers exposed to scams and inflated broker fees, and leaving genuine financers buried under unqualified leads.

Private Finance Solutions was built to replace that uncertainty with a structured, verified, and direct connection between the two sides — charging low commission compared to outside brokers, with no initial amount and no hidden fees.
